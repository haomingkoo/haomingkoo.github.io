# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, senior engineers, collaborators, and casual visitors evaluating Haoming Koo's applied AI work. They need to understand his role, credibility, shipped products, technical judgment, and fit without already knowing the project history.

## Product Purpose

Make Haoming's real engineering work easy to inspect and remember. The portfolio should turn verified experience, live applications, project evidence, and public-safe architecture into a clear path from first impression to product exploration and contact.

## Brand Personality

Professional, evidence-led, and quietly distinctive. The experience should feel confident and technically rigorous without sounding inflated, generic, or machine-generated.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated cover art that obscures what a project actually looks like.
- Atmospheric visuals presented where visitors expect screenshots, results, or product evidence.
- Repetitive AI portfolio templates, vague capability claims, excessive glow, and decorative motion.
- Dense resume-style walls of text or keyword lists without a clear visitor journey.
- Private screenshots, confidential implementation details, or unsupported performance claims.

## Design Principles

1. Show the real product before describing the stack.
2. Make every important claim inspectable through a screenshot, live link, repository, or public source.
3. Help non-technical visitors understand the user problem while preserving depth for engineers.
4. Use the chatbot as a grounded guide to existing evidence, never as a source of invented claims.
5. Keep names, facts, links, and project descriptions consistent across the website, repositories, and machine-readable surfaces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve visible focus, keyboard operation, semantic headings, meaningful alternative text, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion behavior, and mobile touch targets of at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels.
